# Fictional Northstar Workshop handbook

Source: synthetic demonstration written for Beru. No real course material.
Scope: these rules apply only to the fictional Northstar Workshop.

## Page 1 — Project review

At Northstar Workshop, a project review has three stages: identify the problem, compare possible solutions, and agree on the next action. The facilitator records the agreed action and its owner. A review is complete only when both the action and its owner are recorded.

## Page 2 — Prototype labels

Northstar uses three fictional prototype labels. Amber means an idea is awaiting review. Blue means a prototype is being tested. Green means the team has accepted the prototype for the next internal demonstration. Green does not mean public release.
