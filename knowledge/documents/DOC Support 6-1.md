Applications- Individuals are trained to begin with a fact, statement, or opinion and later add
concepts to back up or explain the conclusion as necessary. The preference is to
begin a message or report with an executive summary or bullet points. Discussions are
approached in a practical, concrete manner. Theoretical or philosophical discussions
are avoided in a business environment.

first
Individuals have been trained to first develop the theory or complex concept before
presenting a fact, statement, or opinion. The preference is to begin a message or
report by building up a theoretical argument before moving on to a conclusion. The
conceptual principles underlying each situation are valued.

Linear-time

## Visually verified chart comparisons and distinctions

Page 1: The US lies toward low-context communication; Japan toward high-context. France is more high-context than the US, but France is closer to direct negative feedback than the US. Communication implicitness and negative-feedback directness are separate scales. Direct negative feedback may occur in front of a group and uses absolute descriptors; indirect negative feedback is private and softened by positive messages and qualifiers.

Page 2: France is toward principles-first persuasion; the US toward applications-first. Principles-first develops theory before facts and conclusions. Applications-first begins with a fact, opinion, executive summary or bullet points and adds concepts as necessary. Egalitarian leadership has low boss-subordinate distance, a facilitator among equals and flat structures. Hierarchical leadership has high distance, a strong director, important status and fixed hierarchical communication lines.

Pages 2–3: Japan lies toward hierarchical leadership on page 2 but toward consensual decision-making on page 3. Hierarchical leadership does not imply top-down decision-making. Consensual decisions require unanimous group agreement; top-down decisions are made by an individual, usually the boss. These charts describe comparative cultural tendencies, not every individual's behavior.

Page 3: Task-based trust follows consistent good work and reliability, with business relationships built or dropped according to practicality. Relationship-based trust grows slowly through shared meals, personal time and trusted connections. The US is toward task-based trust and Saudi Arabia toward relationship-based trust.

Page 4: Confrontational disagreement treats open debate as positive and compatible with preserving the relationship. Avoiding confrontation treats open disagreement as a threat to group harmony. Germany is toward confrontational disagreement; Japan toward avoiding confrontation. Linear-time completes one task before the next, rejects interruptions and values deadlines and organization over flexibility. Flexible-time accepts interruptions, handles many tasks at once and values adaptability over organization. Germany lies toward linear-time; Saudi Arabia toward flexible-time.
