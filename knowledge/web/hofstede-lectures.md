# Hofstede: online lectures

Source: https://geerthofstede.com/training-consulting/online-lectures/

### 10 minutes with Geert Hofstede

Listen to Geert explain his work and provide context. In 2015, together with Marcel Siegmund and Iekje Smit, and adopted by the [Geert Hofstede consortium,](http://www.masterinternationalcommunication.eu/) Geert created a serious of tutorial 10-minute videos with PowerPoint slides. You can find the videos below and the slides on the right-hand side or further down. There is an introduction video plus one of each per dimension of national culture.
