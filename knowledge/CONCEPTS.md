## Retrieval cautions

Hofstede and Trompenaars share some terminology but define different frameworks. Organizational culture is not interchangeable with national culture. The two study guides are derivative summaries of the original PDFs, not independent corroboration. Video anecdotes illustrate experiences rather than population-wide statistical facts. An answer choice containing “always” or “all individuals” may overstate a national or generational tendency.
